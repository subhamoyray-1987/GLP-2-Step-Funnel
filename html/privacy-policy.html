Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 25, 2026

1. Introduction

At VitalPath Medical, we are deeply committed to protecting the privacy and security of our patients. We recognize that medical information is highly personal and sensitive. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information through our digital platform and telehealth services.

Our commitment to privacy is not just a legal requirement but a cornerstone of our clinical excellence. We adhere to the highest industry standards to ensure that your path to vitality remains confidential and secure.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Identification

Full name, date of birth, government-issued ID for identity verification, contact information (email, phone), and shipping address.

Health Information

Medical history, current symptoms, allergy profiles, laboratory test results, and clinical photography for diagnostic review.

Technical Data

IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction patterns with our digital interface to optimize performance.

Payment Information

Securely processed payment tokens. We do not store raw credit card data on our servers.

3. How We Use Information

  • Medical Services: To facilitate clinical consultations between patients and licensed healthcare providers.
  • Clinical Review: Analyzing lab results and medical history to determine treatment eligibility and safety.
  • Communication: Sending prescription updates, appointment reminders, and critical health alerts via secure channels.
  • Compliance: Fulfilling our regulatory obligations as a medical entity, including mandatory reporting where required by law.

4. Data Security Standards

We employ enterprise-grade security protocols to safeguard your data. This includes:

AES-256 Encryption

Encryption at rest and in transit for all patient data.

SOC2 Hosting

Data stored on secure, geographically redundant server clusters.

Access Control

Strict multi-factor authentication for all clinical staff access.

5. HIPAA Compliance

VitalPath Medical is a "Covered Entity" under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). All Protected Health Information (PHI) is handled according to stringent HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules. We enter into Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with all technical partners who may have incidental access to infrastructure handling PHI.

6. Third-Party Services

We share information with third parties only as necessary to provide our services:

Pharmacy Partners Diagnostic Labs Payment Processors

Note: We do not sell your personal or health information to third-party advertisers.

7. Your Rights

You have specific rights regarding your personal and health data:

Right to Access: You may request a copy of your medical records and personal data at any time.

Right to Correction: You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information in your profile.

Right to Deletion: You may request account deletion, subject to state and federal medical record retention requirements.

Right to Restrict Processing: You may request that we limit how we use your health data for specific non-clinical purposes.
